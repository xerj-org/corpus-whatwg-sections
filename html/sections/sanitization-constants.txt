WHATWG HTML — section sanitization-constants — Sanitization constants

  <h4>Sanitization constants</h4>

  <p>An element's <dfn>sanitization category</dfn> can be one of the following:</p>

  <dl>
   <dt><dfn data-x="sanitizer-category-default">Default</dfn></dt>
   <dd>The element is included in the default <code>SanitizerConfig</code>.</dd>

   <dt><dfn data-x="sanitizer-category-unsafe">Unsafe</dfn></dt>
   <dd>The element can result in XSS. (Such elements are preserved by <code
   data-x="dom-Element-setHTMLUnsafe">setHTMLUnsafe()</code> or <code
   data-x="dom-parseHTMLUnsafe">parseHTMLUnsafe()</code>, and removed by <code
   data-x="dom-Element-setHTML">setHTML()</code>, <code data-x="dom-parseHTML">parseHTML()</code>,
   and <code data-x="dom-Sanitizer-removeUnsafe">removeUnsafe()</code>.)</dd>

   <dt><dfn data-x="sanitizer-uncategorized">Uncategorized</dfn></dt>
   <dd>The element is neither removed nor included by default, but can still be added to a
   <code>SanitizerConfig</code>.</dd>
  </dl>

  <p>The <dfn>built-in safe baseline configuration</dfn> is a <span>SanitizerConfig</span>. Its
  <code data-x="dom-SanitizerConfig-removeElements">removeElements</code> list consists of all HTML
  elements normatively marked as <span data-x="sanitizer-category-unsafe">Unsafe</span> within their
  individual definitions, along with the obsolete <code>frame</code> element, and the <span>SVG
  <code>script</code></span> and <span>SVG <code>use</code></span> elements, and its <code
  data-x="dom-SanitizerConfig-removeAttributes">removeAttributes</code> list is empty.</p>

  <p class="note">Event handler content attributes are automatically removed by the <span>remove
  unsafe</span> algorithm during safe sanitization, so the effective baseline behaves as if they
  were included in the <code data-x="dom-SanitizerConfig-removeAttributes">removeAttributes</code>
  list.</p>
