  <h4>The <code>meter</code> element</h4>

  <pre><code class="css" data-x="">@namespace "http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml";

meter { appearance: auto; }</code></pre>

  <div algorithm>
  <p>The <code>meter</code> element is a <span>devolvable widget</span>. Its <span>native
  appearance</span> is <span>expected</span> to render as an <span>'inline-block'</span> box with a
  <span>'block-size'</span> of '1em' and an <span>'inline-size'</span> of '5em', a
  <span>'vertical-align'</span> of '-0.2em', and with its contents depicting a gauge.</p>
  </div>

  <p>When this element has a <span>horizontal writing mode</span>, the depiction is
  <span>expected</span> to be of a horizontal gauge. Its minimum value is on the right if the
  <span>'direction'</span> property has a <span>computed value</span> of 'rtl', and on the left
  otherwise. When this element has a <span>vertical writing mode</span>, it is
  <span>expected</span> to depict a vertical gauge. Its minimum value is on the bottom if the
  <span>'direction'</span> property has a <span>computed value</span> of 'rtl', and on the top
  otherwise.</p>

  <p>User agents are <span>expected</span> to use a presentation consistent with platform
  conventions for gauges, if any.</p>

  <p class="note">Requirements for what <!--non-normative-->must be depicted in the gauge are
  included in the definition of the <code>meter</code> element.</p>
